# PRODUCT.md

> Product context for the Impeccable design skill. Hand-authored (no interactive init).

## Product

**PathwayEd** — an AI-powered K–12 tutoring app. A friendly tutor named **Nikki**
helps kids practice math, reading, writing, and science, guiding them toward the
answer instead of just handing it over. Moving from a vendor-fair demo toward a
real student pilot (Claude tutor, Supabase persistence, parent consent).

## Audience

Two distinct users, each with their own surfaces:

1. **Parents / guardians — setting up and overseeing.** They sign in (SSO), give
   consent, add their children, and review progress. Surfaces should feel
   trustworthy, calm, and quick to get through. Plain language, no jargon.
2. **K–12 kids — learning.** They practice with Nikki. Surfaces should feel warm,
   encouraging, and age-appropriate, with large friendly targets and clear
   next steps. Never collect personal info from children.

## Positioning & voice

- **Supportive, not flashy.** Calm, encouraging, quietly competent — a helpful
  tutor, not a hype machine.
- **Guides, doesn't give.** Nikki leads kids to answers with hints and questions.
- **Safe by default.** Age-appropriate, school-safe, escalates distress to a
  trusted adult; reminds kids it can be wrong.
- **Parent-respecting.** Clear about what's collected and why; consent first.
